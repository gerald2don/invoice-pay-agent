Event ledger — a chain of custody for every dollar

Every invoice, count, diagnosis, claim, credit, and PayPal payout in this app is written to one append-only log. Each event carries the SHA-256 hash of the event before it, and its own hash is computed over that link plus its contents — so any alteration, deletion, or reordering breaks the chain at a specific, visible point. This is not a public blockchain and claims no network or token: it is the same tamper-evidence idea kept as one honest local log, in a format that is chain-ready if the history ever needs to anchor to one.
