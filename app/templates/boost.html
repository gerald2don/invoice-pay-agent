Ad Boosting — every post can be boosted; the numbers help you choose

What boosting is: paying the ad platform to show one of the store's posts to more people. Every post can be boosted. The app rates each one and estimates what a boost would likely do — the manager makes the call. Pick any post below, set the budget and the days, approve — and PayPal pays the ad platform, on the same connection that pays the suppliers. Boosts are managed right here: start one, watch it, end it. Every spend lands in the same ledger as every other dollar.

How the rating works: reach counts how many people the post already found, engagement rate how they reacted, and store visits per 1,000 reach how many walked in. Strong = 10,000+ reach with strong reaction and visits; Decent = 5,000+ reach with solid numbers; Light = it ran, but the response was thin; Too new to tell = under 1,000 reach so far. How the estimate works: estimated extra reach is a multiple of the post's own organic reach, wider for stronger ratings because the platform steers paid reach toward likely responders — scaled by budget and run length.
