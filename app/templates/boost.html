Ad Boosting — pay to repeat what already worked

What boosting is: paying the ad platform to show one of the store's posts to more people. This store's rule: only a post pattern the store's own results have already proven can be boosted — the numbers below are from the store's own past posts, and they decide what's eligible. An unproven post can't be boosted at any price, so ad money never follows a hunch. The manager picks a proven pattern, sets the budget and the days, approves — and PayPal pays the ad platform, on the same connection that pays the suppliers. The spend lands in the same ledger as every other dollar.
