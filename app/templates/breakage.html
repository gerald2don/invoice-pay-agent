Breakage — log a loss

Something breaks in a store every week: a case dropped in the back room, a bottle burst in the cooler. If that loss is never written down, it hides until a count comes up short — and gets lumped in with theft, which poisons the shrink number the owner actually watches. Log it here instead, by hand or with a photo, and the product comes out of inventory on the spot. Every loss is priced at cost and written to the same ledger as every dollar, so the known losses stay separate from the shrink nobody can explain. A defective item forks at the same door: out of inventory the same way, but routed to returns — it goes back to the vendor instead of into the breakage bucket.
