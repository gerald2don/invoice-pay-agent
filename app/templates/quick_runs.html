Quick Runs — the errand loop

Stores run out of things their distributors don't bring — cleaning supplies, bags, cups, receipt tape — and today that means someone leaves the floor, drives to the store, and loses the receipt in a pocket. A Quick Run closes that loop: the shift checklist flags what's out, the agent builds the cart at the store's usual spot-buy source, a manager approves, and PayPal pays. Delivery is tracked, and the receipt files into the same books as an invoice — the errand becomes a record.

🏷️ Owner rule — small-dollar auto-approve: runs at or under ${{ "%.2f"|format(limit) }} are pre-approved by the owner. They go on their own and the manager gets the notice after the fact — the limit is the human's standing yes. Above ${{ "%.2f"|format(limit) }}, a run waits for a manager's tap, like everything else in this app. Nothing charges without one of those two yeses.
